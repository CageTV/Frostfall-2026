Frostfall 2026 @VERSION@ - Frostfall.dll update

Install this over Frostfall 2026 4.1.x (it replaces SKSE/Plugins/Frostfall.dll and nothing else). It asks which build you have, Regular or ESL, and which
DLL fits your game: "new" for Skyrim 1.6.1170 and newer, "older" for Skyrim VR and 1.6.1130 and older (on 1.6.1170 either works).

WHAT IS NEW
-----------
The HUD bars can be laid out the way you like (settings: SKSE Menu Framework, Frostfall, HUD):
- Direction: standing bars or bars lying flat.
- Flip: fill from the other end (the top, or the right); the icons move to that end.
- Layout: 2 x 2 grid, a single row (a single column when the bars lie flat), or Free, where every bar sits anywhere on the screen.
- Free layout: each bar has its own position, direction, flip, length and thickness.
- Unlock a bar (or, in the grid and row layouts, the whole group) and drag it with the mouse, then lock it again.
- The HUD page shows the bars with sample values before Frostfall has started, so they can be placed at any time.

Last Seed 2026 1.3.0 and Stress and Fear 2026 1.4.0 have the same controls; Last Seed stacks under Frostfall's bars in whatever direction they use.

The full Frostfall 2026 install (scripts, plugin, installer) is still 4.1.1 / the 4.1.0 files; this update needs them.
