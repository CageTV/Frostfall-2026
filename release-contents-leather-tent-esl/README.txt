Frostfall 2026 - Leather Tent
Version 1.0.0, ESL BUILD, an optional add-on for Frostfall 2026 (ESL) (Frostfall 4.0.0 pre-release, an update layer for Chesko's Frostfall)

WHAT IT DOES
------------
Adds a second makeshift camp to the "Make Camp" menu, the leather camp. It costs 4 Branches, 1 Linen Wrap and 2 Leather (the
simple camp is 4 Branches and 2 Linen Wraps) and gives better insulation: while you sit or lie in it, the shelter bonus is
100 instead of 80, one exposure level more, so cold, wind and snow reach you less than in the simple camp.

Everything else works like the simple camp: wear an Adventurer's Backpack with a bedroll, light a campfire, activate the fire and
pick "Leather Camp". The camp is used once, falls apart by itself a game week after it was set up (unless you are at it), is taken
down when you destroy its campfire, and leaves nothing behind. On ground too uneven for the camp to be placed, it says so and uses
up nothing.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
- Frostfall 2026 4.0.0 (ESL) (this add-on only adds to its Make Camp menu) and everything that needs, including the ESL
  Campfire. It does not work with the normal Frostfall 2026: the records it points at have other FormIDs there.
- The same Creation Club content as Make Camp (Adventurer's Backpacks and Camping).

INSTALL
-------
Install with your mod manager and put it AFTER Frostfall 2026 (below it in MO2's left pane; its plugin loads after
Frostfall.esp). The plugin is ESL-flagged: it takes no regular plugin slot.

UNINSTALL
---------
Remove it with your mod manager. A leather camp that is standing in your save should be taken down first (destroy its campfire).
